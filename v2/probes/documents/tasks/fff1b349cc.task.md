Let one of this station's ships that has an owner pair another peer's ship without anyone typing a pairing code, but only within a live owner trust that a person granted, and only when every check holds. Nothing is written until they all pass, and each refusal should say why.

Anyone but such a ship, an admin, a station ship with no owner or an unknown account, gets 403 before anything else is looked at. The ship's card must be well formed (400), come from a peer a person already paired here with its code, still paired and never retired (409), and match that paired card in everything but the account it identifies (409). The ship's signed assertion must verify against that card's key and be in date, and name exactly the account the card identifies, its id and its name (400). The owner is the contact with the assertion's peer and owner id together, never the id alone; its code must have been typed and still be current, and it must not be held (409), with a reason that makes clear it is the owner that failed. A live trust must cover that owner: none, a revoked one, or one from its end time on gets 403, though one second before its end still works, and its ending leaves the peer and the ships it paired in place. The assertion must date from strictly after the trust's last revocation, even under a newer trust (403, explaining why). The ship's name must be 2 to 32 lowercase letters, digits and dashes, starting with a letter (400), and match no local account's login name or display name and no person contact's name by full Unicode case-folding (409); the same name on another peer is fine. The ship must not already be a contact here, even with a person contact's id (409).

Each trust pairs at most 200 ships a UTC day. The next gets 429 with a reason that gives the limit of 200, and a new UTC day starts afresh. Cap refusals are tallied per trust per day, in their own transaction after the pairing's rolls back; no other refusal writes anything. Run the checks in one transaction that takes the write lock first, so two pairings at once at 199 pair exactly one.

A pairing adds one contact on the ship's peer, with the ship's id and its own name, not held, and one confirmation record of the grant, the acting ship, the signed assertion as canonical JSON, the time and the UTC day; it reports that the trust, not a typed code, vouched for it, and which grant. Give every contact one display form, there for any screen to use: a contact paired by a trust reads as its owner's ship, the owner's current name then the ship's name, even once revoked or held, and an owner's rename shows; a person reads as their own name. The new command below shows a newly paired ship by it.

Add a command a ship runs with no terminal and nothing on standard input, given the data directory, the card file, the assertion file and its own account. It reads each file strictly, at most 6 MiB per file. A missing file, a directory, a file that is not JSON, one over 6 MiB or not a valid assertion, no peer database, or an account that is a person or does not exist each ends it with exit 1, nothing on standard output, and a single safe error line on standard error whose explanation of why is limited to 200 characters, the command's own prefix outside that bound. None of these writes anything, though a refusal at the daily cap still records its tally. Success exits 0, leaves standard error empty, and prints one line giving the new contact's display form and saying the trust vouched for it. It never prints a pairing code.
