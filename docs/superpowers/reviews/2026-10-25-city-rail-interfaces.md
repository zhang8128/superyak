The rules I worked by:

- P is the source of truth for what a permission means. I is the source of truth for what leaves a machine. T is the
  source of truth for routing.
- Where privacy and trail quality conflict, privacy wins, and T records the cost.
- Every decision below that is not Andy's is Rio Grande's call. Each spec marks it so where it is made.
- Section numbers are cited, not line numbers, because lines move. Code is cited by file and line, read today.

Nothing here is built. I committed nothing, pushed nothing and posted nothing.
