# Experiment E1: does use carry information that meaning doesn't?

2026-11-03. Plan: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-rail-mathematics.md` (sections 5 and 6), as revised after review. Script: `scripts/rail_e1.py`. Numbers only: rooms appear by short id and authors by kind (person, ship or system). No message text and no name appears here.

## Why the plan's first design was not run

Each message is written once, in one session, so the plan's trail graph over messages is a disjoint union of paths. Measured on this corpus, with duplicates and the same split:

- trained edges: 2065; the largest support of any edge: 1; the largest out-degree of any message: 1;
- held-out steps: 251; held-out next messages that are trained nodes: 0; held-out current messages that are trained nodes: 0.

So arm (ii) as first written could never rank the true next message, and C2 had a single bucket. The revision quotients the sphere into places, so places recur and support grows past 1.

## Pre-registration

**Pre-registered falsifier.** I fixed it on 2026-11-03 before any E1 number was computed. I revised it three times that
day, each before any number from the station's vectors existed:

- twice on the coordinator's refinements, which name the place count `P` with 64 primary, take (iv) from another
  author, and restrict the test to place-changing steps with the current place dropped;
- once on a smoke run with random vectors and the real sessions. There (ii) beat (i), (iv), (v) and (vi) with no
  sequence information at all, through the base rate of places, which ranking by transition count carries and those
  four arms don't. So the popularity arm (vii) joins the max. The coordinator's rule is "C1 holds only if (ii) beats
  max(i, iv, v, vi)", and this adds a necessary condition without removing one.

- **Population.** Held-out **place-changing, non-reply steps**: session steps `a → b` where `place(b) ≠ place(a)` and
  `b` does not answer `a`.
- **Rankings.** Each arm's ranking of places, with the current place `place(a)` removed. Then hit@10.
- **Statistic.** `D = hit@10(ii) − max(hit@10(i), hit@10(iv), hit@10(v), hit@10(vi), hit@10(vii))`. Each hit@10 is
  the mean over the population. The max is taken over the five arms inside every bootstrap resample, so choosing the
  strongest baseline is part of the statistic. `D4`, the same without (vii), is reported beside it.
- **Interval.** A paired bootstrap clustered by held-out session. Each resample draws sessions with replacement and
  keeps all their steps, with one draw for every arm. 1,000 resamples, 95% percentile interval.
- **Primary.** The deduplicated corpus at `P = 64`. **C1 fails if the primary 95% interval includes 0 or lies below
  it.**
- **Secondary**, reported but not deciding: `P = 32` and `P = 128`, the corpus with its duplicates, hit@1 and hit@5,
  all steps including self-steps, `D4`, and the online arm (ii+). A primary pass that doesn't hold at `P = 32` and
  `P = 128` on the deduplicated corpus is reported as fragile, not as a pass.

## Method

- **Corpus.** The station store's `messages` table, opened with `mode=ro`: 2674 messages in 7 rooms by 8 authors (by kind: person 2, ship 2670, system 2). `message_replies` links 1423 messages to the message each answers.
- **Embedding.** The shared embedder only (`RemoteEmbedProvider` over its socket), with the `search_document:` prefix and batches of 16. The service's model truncates at 2048 tokens. Vectors are L2-normalized. The angle is `θ = 2·atan2(‖â−b̂‖, ‖â+b̂‖)`, in float64.
- **Duplicates.** A message within `θ < 0.05` rad of an earlier message by the same author is a templated repeat. There are 6 (by kind: person 0, ship 6, system 0). Both versions run: *with duplicates*, and *deduplicated*, where repeats are removed before sessions are cut.
- **Sessions and split.** A session is a maximal run of one author's messages in one room with gaps under 30 minutes. Steps join consecutive messages. Sessions starting before the 80th percentile of session start times train, and the rest are held out.
- **Places.** Spherical k-means (k-means++ seeding, 4 restarts, the best objective kept) on the training messages only, for `P` in {32, 64, 128}. A message's place is its nearest centroid. Counts and support come from training steps only. Support is the number of distinct training sessions with that place-to-place step.
- **Arms.** Each arm ranks all `P` places. hit@k asks whether `place(b)` is among the first `k`.
  - (i) ranks places by θ from `a` to each centroid.
  - (ii) ranks places by training transition count from `place(a)`, with ties and backfill by θ between centroids.
  - (iv) ranks places by θ from the room's latest message by another author before `b`, falling back to (i) when there is none.
  - (v) and (vi) take the `m = 10` nearest earlier messages to `a` (before `b`, `a` excluded), by the same author for (v) and in the same room for (vi). They vote for their places, with ties broken by the nearest member, then backfill in (i)'s order.
  - (vii) ranks places by how often training steps moved into them from another place, with ties in (i)'s order. It is the base rate that (ii) carries too, and it doesn't depend on `a`.
  - (ii+) is (ii) with counts that also take held-out steps finished before `b`. It is secondary and outside the falsifier.
  - The plan's A* arm measures reachability toward the true next vector, not prediction, so it was dropped.
- **Steps.**
  - A *self-step* stays in its place, and every arm gets it nearly free, since (i) ranks the current place first.
  - On *place-changing* steps every ranking drops the current place before hit@k is taken.
  - *Non-reply* means `b` does not answer `a`.
- **Intervals.** As pre-registered: paired, clustered by held-out session, 1,000 resamples, percentile 95%.

## Reading

This section is written by hand after the run. Everything above it is the script's output.

**C1 fails on this corpus, and the result isn't close to the line.** The pre-registered population is 212
place-changing, non-reply held-out steps in 29 sessions (deduplicated, P = 64). There, use (ii) puts the next place in
its top 10 for 25.5% of steps, against 49.5% for the same-room kNN (vi). So `D@10 = −0.241 [−0.312, −0.193]`.

- **The same verdict everywhere.** (ii) loses at every P, in both versions, and at hit@1, @5 and @10. Every interval
  lies wholly below 0. The coordinator's four-arm `D4` gives the same verdict, so adding the popularity arm (vii)
  decided nothing.
- **Use carries less than meaning, or than the base rate.** (ii) is worse than meaning alone ((i): −0.217
  [−0.279, −0.153] at @10). It's also worse than the bare frequency of places ((vii): −0.184 [−0.293, −0.060]), so the
  transition counts carry less than the frequencies they're built from.
- **Online counts close only part of the gap.** (ii+) reaches 0.373 at @10, still below (i) at 0.472.
- **The chat arms do best.** The author kNN (v) and the room kNN (vi) are the strongest arms. Whatever order the next
  step has is better explained by the author's and the room's recent neighbourhood in meaning than by trained
  transitions.
- **C2 isn't supported.** (ii)'s hit@10 by top-edge support is 0.14, 0.34, 0.33 and 0.17 for supports 1, 2, 3–4 and
  5–9, with no rise. (i) beats (ii) at @10 in every bucket.

By the plan's section 7, the rail work stops on this evidence. What's open is why it failed: the data, the window or
the embedding.

The caveats bear on that why:

- **This data isn't memory use.** 2,670 of the 2,674 messages are by ships, and there are 0 held-out steps by people,
  so the human-only result is empty. A session here is a ship writing new messages, not an agent touching old
  memories. The plan's own exit names this case: rerun E1 on an Engram store once one holds a few hundred memories.
- **The window is short and drifts.** The held-out period is the last 1.8 days (with duplicates) to 2.8 days
  (deduplicated) of a 30-day corpus. Held-out place occupancy differs from training by total variation 0.45–0.57, and
  held-out messages use 46 of the 64 places. Frozen counts suffer most from that, and (ii+) recovers part of it.
- **The transitions are sparse.** There are 2,048 training steps over 64 × 64 place pairs, and the largest support of
  any place-changing step is 7 at P = 64. Such noisy estimates fit (ii) losing even to (vii). A smoothed (ii), counts
  plus a popularity prior, wasn't pre-registered and wasn't run.
- **Deduplication barely bit.** `θ < 0.05` rad caught only 6 messages: templated posts differ in their numbers and ids
  enough to sit further apart. Removing those 6 moved the 80th-percentile cutoff by a day (2026-10-31 22:50 UTC
  deduplicated, 2026-11-01 22:34 UTC with duplicates, the second computed from timestamps alone). So the two versions
  differ mainly in their split, and they agree in their verdict.
- **The corpus grew during the work.** The brief counted 2,629 messages, and the run read the 2,674 present when it
  started.
