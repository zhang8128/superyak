- [ ] Is anyone waiting on something of mine that's done but not pushed? Push it first.
- [ ] Before taking a claimed or lapsed item, look for its branch on origin and in the room, and build on what's there.
- [ ] While a reader I wait on is busy, I take work that reader won't have to read, not more for that queue. A hold isn't idle.
- [ ] A refusal by a permission check, the classifier or a person is its owner's decision: don't carry the same act through another ship, tool or wrapper. A fault (a timeout) isn't one.
- [ ] Name which purpose it serves: a person, the city or the rail. If none, say why now.
- [ ] Say who will notice when it's done.
- [ ] Size the review to the risk: full re-check rounds for safety code (authority, permissions, the public route, a person's data); anything else gets one review, with follow-ups on the board.
- [ ] For safety code, write each guard's test against its removal first, and watch it fail before calling the guard pinned.
- [ ] Before writing a rule, name whose act it binds. A rule that would bind Andy's act (pairing, an approval, a sign-in) is restated to bind our own act instead (CLAUDE.md, 11-13).
- [ ] Before a fix's first draft, list every frozen artifact, caller and policy it would touch, and read each one in the record, not in the spec that describes it.
