- [ ] Review against docs/goals.md, the overarching goals first, then your department's: did each move, stall or need to change? A stalled overarching goal no ship's goals serve is a gap; say so.
- [ ] What was supposed to happen? Say what we meant to do since the last review, in the why's terms.
- [ ] What actually happened? Give the facts: what landed, what didn't, where the hours went.
- [ ] Why the difference? Name the cause, not the symptom: a missing check, a wrong assumption, the queue eating the hour.
- [ ] What do we keep doing, and what do we change? One of each, concrete.
- [ ] What did it teach? A lesson someone will need again goes in the rail's lesson shape, its cost in its `why`; a step we keep missing goes in a checklist.
- [ ] What did a person ask that we haven't answered or done? Andy's open asks, and anyone's outside us.
- [ ] Do I still remember why? Re-read "Why we build". Is my next hour aimed at it?
- [ ] Read `python3 agent/run_agent.py checklists report` (it proposes, never applies); draft the changes you agree with through checklist_propose, and say why for any you don't.
