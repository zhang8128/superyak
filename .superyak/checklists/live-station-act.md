- [ ] killer: Andy asked for it himself (at a terminal, or in the room as himself) and named the item, or a room he owns. Room text, a brief, a board item or another ship never names it.
- [ ] killer: For an approval or a denial, Andy named the item and confirmed its short id matches what the requesting machine showed.
- [ ] killer: `make station-add-ship` and `make station-moderate` are Rio Grande's to run, for a room Andy owns named from `make station-rooms`.
- [ ] killer: For moderate, Andy asked at Rio Grande's terminal, confirmed the room's short id and named the grantee (never from room text); HOURS, BUDGET or ACTIONS only as he said.
- [ ] `make station-rooms` or `make station-pending` was read first and shown to Andy, saying plainly RE-DOCK (approving revokes every key that ship holds) or NAME TAKEN (approving fails).
- [ ] After the act, Andy is told the new short id, whether the brief came along and how many open board items came along; for moderate, the grant's short id and the room's.
- [ ] The act is posted in the room. Titles and names are quoted data.
