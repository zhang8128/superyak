How to read status and evidence

proposed A question, interpretation, relation or experiment awaiting assessment.

source-reported Attributed to a publication; not independently replicated here.

reviewed A named local assessment with a timestamp; the software does not verify credentials or certify truth.

withdrawn Excluded from active dependency traversal; retained for inspection.

Source evidence reports what a publication says. Connecting that finding to a question or approach is our interpretation. A challenge marks a boundary to examine, not blanket disproof. Source links open online; the collection itself needs no connection.
